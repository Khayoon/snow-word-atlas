SNOW — A LANDSCAPE WORD MAP

Double-click "Open Snow Atlas.cmd" to open the map editor.
Node.js is required. No npm install or build step is needed.
The server stays on your computer (127.0.0.1). Close its window to stop.

MAKE AN IMAGE
The image is the main deliverable: a flat 1800:1100 landscape composition,
continuous colour on land, native words, and a legend in the upper left.
There is no sidebar in the artwork.
Non-Latin words have a parenthesized romanized reading beneath them.

Choose PNG at 6,000, 9,000 or 12,000 pixels wide, or editable SVG.
The default PNG is 6000 x 3667 pixels. Controls and dialogs are excluded.
Search highlights are cleared in export. Colour mode, label size, layer
settings and map composition remain part of the exported image.

Use the word-size slider to adjust density. Drag or zoom to compose.
Reset composition restores the full region. Click a word to read its
etymology and sources in a dialog, or choose a language from the dropdown.

COLOURS AND LEGEND
The default routes view gives different colours to Germanic, Latin/Romance,
Slavic, Baltic and Goidelic routes beneath their shared PIE snow root.
Other established or unresolved word-origin sets stay separate.
Shared-roots mode gives the PIE snow cognates a single colour.
Language-branch mode compares general family relationships instead.

An asterisk marks reconstruction. Modern forms without an asterisk in the
legend can identify an attested comparative set without asserting a deeper
origin. Histories are schematic; source notes identify unresolved analyses.

CONTINUOUS LAND COVERAGE
Full Natural Earth country land provides a reference-language background.
Published minority-language polygons sit above it. This fills the gaps in
speaker-footprint datasets, including sparsely populated land and deserts.
It does not turn the map into a new ethnic or census boundary survey.

All source land inside [-27,25,68,75] is covered, including Sinai, the Zagros,
eastern Turkey, North Caucasus, northern Russia and the Karakum Desert.
Generalized source areas overlap; some supplements show traditional ranges.
Some selected varieties have point labels without a separate area polygon.
Leader lines connect crowded labels to their anchors.

The lexicon has 101 selected entries and 25 deeper word-origin sets.
The default composition places 97 word labels; not every selected variety
has a separate territory or fits inside the crop. These are selected words
and varieties, not an exhaustive dictionary of every dialect or synonym.

EDITABLE DATA
dist/data/lexicon.json: words, roots, historical stages, notes and references.
dist/data/territories.geojson: published language-area polygons.
dist/data/land-reference.geojson: complete background reference-language land.
dist/data/map-coverage.json: coverage method, provenance and spatial checks.
dist/data/geometry-sources.json: atlas sources, licenses and transformations.
dist/app.js and styles.css: composition, labels, legend and export logic.

All libraries and data files are local. Dictionary links need internet.
